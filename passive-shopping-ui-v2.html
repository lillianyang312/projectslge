What's New in v2

Upload

AI Clarification Flow

When the AI can't confidently identify an item, it shows a friendly clarification card with suggested options. Users can tap a suggestion or type their own. This reduces bad categorization without adding friction to clear uploads.

Agent

Agent-Managed Logistics

After a deal is confirmed, the agent coordinates pickup times or shipping labels. New screens for Pickup (local) and Shipping with timelines, tracking, and status updates. The agent suggests meeting spots based on both parties' preferences.

Chat

Conversations from Matches

Chat is now accessible from the Matches and Deals flow. The agent participates in the chat to coordinate logistics, but buyer/seller can also message directly. Quick actions for common situations (running late, reschedule, question).

Navigation

Deals Tab Replaces Matches

The bottom nav now has "Deals" instead of separate Matches. Deals tab shows Active transactions, Matches (mutual interest), and History. This consolidates all transaction states in one place.

Swipe (Sell)

Smart Sell Recommendation

Left side now shows "Our take" (Sell now / Wait / Counter) based on market trajectory and the seller's indicated urgency. Right side shows the current max offer with market estimate context. Much clearer decision-making for sellers.
